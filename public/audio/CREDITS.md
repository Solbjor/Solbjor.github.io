# Audio credits

NASA material is generally not subject to copyright in the United States. Use follows the NASA Media Usage Guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/multimedia/guidelines/index.html (linked from the sounds page). This site isn't affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
